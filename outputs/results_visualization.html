Worth a look: |m| is already down near its ~1/√N finite-size noise floor (N=15,000 spins ⇒ ≈0.008) by T≈100–150 K, well below the 214 K reference — the effective model's order–disorder crossover in this scan appears to sit lower than the full model's Tc, consistent with the scan script's own note that "the effective model may differ." Worth checking against the energy curve's inflection point before treating 214 K as confirmed for this model.
